# Climate Analytics & Environmental Data Science

A data-driven climate analytics project focused on transforming complex environmental and climate datasets into reproducible, interpretable insights using **Python, R, statistical modelling, machine learning, spatial analysis, and scientific visualization**.

The project brings together techniques from data science, hydrology, climate science, and geospatial analysis to investigate patterns and variability in environmental datasets. Particular emphasis is placed on developing reproducible analytical workflows for working with multidimensional climate data, including **NetCDF-based datasets**, spatial-temporal data processing, exploratory analysis, statistical modelling, clustering, classification, and visualization.

## Key Areas

* **Climate & Environmental Data Processing**

  * Working with multidimensional and spatial-temporal datasets
  * Processing NetCDF and other scientific data formats
  * Data cleaning, transformation, aggregation, and quality assessment
  * Handling large environmental datasets efficiently

* **Exploratory Data Analysis**

  * Identifying temporal and spatial patterns
  * Statistical summaries and distribution analysis
  * Correlation and dependency analysis
  * Detection of anomalies and environmental variability

* **Spatial & Geospatial Analytics**

  * Spatial data manipulation and visualization
  * Mapping environmental variables
  * Raster and vector-based analysis
  * Integration of geographic information with climate datasets

* **Statistical & Machine Learning Methods**

  * Regression and predictive modelling
  * Regularization techniques such as **Ridge and Lasso regression**
  * Model evaluation using metrics such as MSE and cross-validation
  * Classification and unsupervised learning
  * Density-based clustering, including **HDBSCAN**

* **Climate Data Classification & Clustering**

  * Identifying distinct environmental regimes
  * Grouping observations based on climatic characteristics
  * Exploring spatial and temporal heterogeneity
  * Comparing machine-learning-derived clusters with physical/environmental interpretation

* **Scientific Visualization**

  * Climate and environmental maps
  * Spatial distributions
  * Time-series analysis
  * Statistical plots and model diagnostics
  * Communicating complex environmental patterns through clear visualizations

## Project Philosophy

The central objective is not simply to apply machine-learning algorithms to climate data, but to establish a workflow in which **data processing, statistical reasoning, machine learning, spatial analysis, and environmental interpretation are connected**.

The project therefore emphasizes:

**Raw Climate Data → Data Processing → Exploratory Analysis → Statistical Analysis → Machine Learning → Spatial Interpretation → Visualization → Environmental Insight**

This approach makes the analyses reproducible while maintaining a strong connection between computational results and the underlying environmental processes.

## Future Development

The project can be extended toward more advanced **climate-risk analytics, investment targets for each cities to achieve 2035 and 2050 goals, spatial machine learning, climate forecasting, remote-sensing integration, and explainable AI for environmental applications**.

Ultimately, the repository serves as a portfolio demonstrating how modern **data science and machine learning can be applied to climate and environmental research**, with an emphasis on reproducibility, statistical rigor, spatial reasoning, and meaningful scientific interpretation.
